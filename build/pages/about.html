Our story

Born from reflection,
built for service

Across Africa, and in Nigeria in particular, poverty, weak education, poor healthcare, limited research and a decline in everyday kindness remain widespread — often in places where resources are abundant. The gap is not only material; it is also a gap in understanding.

Al-Haqqu l-Mubeen Arabic and Islamic Relations grew out of the collective reflection of Muslim scholars and intellectuals who believe that seeking and spreading the true teachings of Islam frees people from misconception, misinterpretation and the deliberate distortion of scripture. Where knowledge is sound, compassion and justice follow.

AHM channels Islamic philanthropy — sadaqah, zakat, grants and endowments — into practical work: schools and students supported, research revived, dialogue opened between institutions, and care extended to orphans, widows and families in need.

Strategic approach

How we work

Our approach is practical and long-term: create the occasion to learn, bring the right people into the room, then follow through.

[ 01 ]

Learning beyond the classroom

  • Extracurricular activities and competitions — debates, public speaking, essay writing, Qur'an recitation, Islamic arts, drama and storytelling.
  • Mentorship and guidance from teachers, scholars and older students.
  • Clubs and associations that keep learning alive between terms.
[ 02 ]

Scholarship in dialogue

  • Inter-school seminars with invited experts from within and beyond the region.
  • Structured follow-up after every seminar, so ideas become projects.
  • Forums, guest speakers and student-led discussions on contemporary issues.
[ 03 ]

Research & heritage

  • Research projects with access to books, journals and academic databases.
  • Support for student-led heritage projects and the preservation of manuscripts.
  • Publication and dissemination of findings for teachers and students.
[ 04 ]

Reaching the wider community

  • Ramadan and weekly radio programmes produced with students and community partners.
  • Public awareness and fundraising for orphans, widows and the needy.
  • Family picnics and social gatherings that strengthen trust between households.
