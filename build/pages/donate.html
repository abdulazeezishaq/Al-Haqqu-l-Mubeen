Zakat & sadaqah

Your zakat is welcome here

AHM distributes zakat only to eligible recipients under the categories named in the Qur'an — including orphans, widows and families in genuine need. Choose Orphans & Widows or Where Most Needed when you give.

Ways to give

Three ways to sustain the work

Give once, give monthly, or help build something that outlasts all of us — an endowment whose income supports the mission year after year.

[ 01 ]

Waqf (Endowment)

AHM acquires landed property and other assets whose income sustains the mission for the long term. A waqf continues to give on your behalf long after the initial gift.

[ 02 ]

Fundraising & Voluntary Contributions

Individuals, families, mosques and organisations can give online or in person — one-time gifts, monthly support, or a fundraising drive for a specific programme.
